# For visitors and players

The public Logi site is for discovering Hell Let Loose communities and the information they choose to publish. You do not need to sign in to browse it.

## What is public?

Public pages may show community members, clan information, player profiles, matches, results, and competition information. The exact data depends on what each community has published and on the information available to Logi.

<Callout type="warning">
    A public profile or match page is a view, not a management console. Sign in
    and open the dashboard when you need to change server information.
</Callout>
