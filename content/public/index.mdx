# For visitors and players

The public Logi site is for discovering Hell Let Loose communities and the information they choose to publish. You do not need to sign in to browse it.

## What is public?

Public pages intentionally expose a limited projection of Logi data. They may
show community identity, active-member counts, recorded match history, player
match statistics, and competition fixtures. They do not expose Discord IDs,
platform IDs, member notes, assignments, unpublished rosters, private Discord
configuration, or management controls.

Public visibility is currently data-driven rather than a per-page publishing
switch. A clan can appear once it has at least one active member (or a recorded
member ID), and a player profile appears only after qualifying recorded match
statistics exist. A planned match is not public merely because it was created.

The exact information on an eligible page depends on the records Logi has for
that player, clan, match, or competition.

<Callout type="warning">
    A public profile or match page is a view, not a management console. Sign in
    and open the dashboard when you need to change server information.
</Callout>
