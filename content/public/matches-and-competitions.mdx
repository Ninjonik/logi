# Matches and competitions

## Public match pages

Open a public match to see the information its community has made available: participating sides, planned or completed time, map information, and results when recorded. A match without a result is not necessarily cancelled—it may simply not have been concluded yet.

## Competitions

The public competitions area lists supported competitions and their visible fixtures. Open a competition to follow its schedule and match links. Competition rules are owned by the competition organiser; use the official rules link when one is provided.

<Steps>

### Find a match

Use the Community page, a clan page, or a competition fixture to open a public match.

### Check the state

Read the date, event status, and result separately. Each answers a different question: when it is/was scheduled, where it is in the workflow, and whether a score has been recorded.

### Need to change the match?

Only authorised server managers can edit the underlying event in their workspace dashboard.

</Steps>

<Callout type="warning">
    A public match link does not grant access to private briefings, rosters,
    Discord settings, or management actions.
</Callout>
