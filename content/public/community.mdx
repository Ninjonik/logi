# Community, clans, and players

## Community directory

The **Community** page brings together discoverable players, clans, and recent public matches. Use it to explore rather than to administer a server. Long lists are split across pages; use the page controls to continue browsing.

## Clan pages

A clan page presents public identity, active-member count, and recorded match
history. It does not expose the member list itself. A clan is eligible for the
directory once Logi sees at least one active assignment or recorded member ID.
A newly created, empty workspace therefore will not appear, while a
competition-only placeholder can also be absent until it has real membership
data.

Clan match statistics use only non-training events with a linked imported match
record. Win rate counts only victory/defeat decisions; draws do not count as a
win or loss decision. The page shows a limited recent-match list, not an
exhaustive operational history.

A clan page presents the clan’s public identity and relevant public activity. It may link to its players and matches. Information is only as current as the community’s Logi records and connections.

## Player profiles

A player page is created only when the player has at least one qualifying
non-training match-stat record linked to an event. The profile includes only
active-clan links and a limited recent-match/statistical summary; it does not
expose Discord IDs, platform IDs, membership assignments, notes, or unpublished
roster data.

A player page identifies a player and can show their public game or community data. It is not an invitation to edit another person’s account. Players manage their own account settings after signing in; server managers manage server-specific membership separately.

<Callout type="info">
    Can’t find a player or clan? It may not be public, it may not have been
    imported or linked yet, or you may need a more specific name.
</Callout>
