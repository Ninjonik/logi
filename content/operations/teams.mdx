# Team profile

A team is a reusable profile for one game. The same team can enter several
competitions for that game, with a separate registration and fixtures in each.
You do not need a Discord server or Logi bot to own and manage a team.

## Create a team from a competition

Open **Leagues**, select a competition and choose **Register team**. Select an
existing team or use **Create team and register**. The competition determines
the game; there is no game picker in this form. Creating a new team also submits
its registration. Automatic competitions accept it immediately; approval-based
competitions show **Awaiting approval**.

If the new team has too few players, Logi saves the profile and explicitly says
that registration is not complete. Copy the player invitation, invite enough
players, then register the existing team. Closed and unpublished competitions
do not create teams through this registration form.

You can also create a standalone profile under **My league teams**, choosing its
game there, or create and connect one directly from your clan's **Team profile**.
An account can own up to five basic profiles. Existing catalogue names cannot
be claimed by creating a duplicate; ask an organiser to resolve an existing
unowned catalogue profile rather than creating another copy.

## Edit branding and invite players

Open **My league teams** from the public account menu or dashboard sidebar.
Clan administrators can instead open **Team profile** in their clan navigation.
**Edit profile** changes the name, short code, description and avatar immediately,
without requesting approval or installing the bot. Team owners and current
administrators of the linked clan can edit; ordinary players cannot.

Names are unique within the game's global catalogue. Upload PNG, JPEG or WebP
up to 2 MiB for the avatar; animated images are rejected. Logi normalizes the
image to at most 512 by 512 pixels. Save the profile to apply the upload, or use
**Remove avatar** and save. Conflicting edits require a refresh. The game's
identity, ownership and Discord link are not editable branding fields.
Existing match snapshots keep their recorded name and logo.

The owner can copy the private player invitation. Players open it, sign in with
Discord and explicitly choose **Join team**. They count once toward league
minimums and gain no team-management or clan-workspace permission.

## Connect the team to a Discord clan

In the team's profile, expand **Discord connection** and select a workspace you
administer. You must own the team and currently have workspace administrator
access. Each Discord server can represent one team across games; occupied
servers cannot be selected. An already connected team cannot be moved through
this action. No bot installation is required for linking.

On a clan's empty **Team profile** page, choose **Create and connect team** or
connect an existing owned team. Dashboard entry also offers linking when an
unlinked team and an available workspace exist. New teams can use these same
connection controls later.

Linking keeps the team's ID, players, invitations, registrations and fixtures.
The owner retains management and linked-clan administrators gain management
of that team's profile and participation. Invited players and signed-in clan
members count once each; invitation membership does not grant clan access.
Legacy competition adoption preserves extra game teams and their history in
the catalogue without attaching them to an occupied server.

## Competitions for your clan

Open **Competitions** in the clan sidebar. **Your competitions** lists current
registered and pending entries first, with status, withdrawal and a link to
registration and fixture management. **Join a competition** lists other
published competitions suitable for the connected team's game. A separate
history section keeps rejected applications and withdrawn entries available.

The same registration is used on the public league page and in the dashboard.
Joining another competition reuses the team. Withdrawing affects only that
competition and retains its results and fixtures. Player minimums and organiser
approval still apply, and organisers assign divisions.

## Team requests have been replaced

Clan administrators create and edit their own team profiles directly. The old
catalogue request form, change-request form and match-editor request action
are no longer available. The former workspace request endpoint returns 410.
Global administrators retain historical requests and catalogue moderation.
Shared opponent selection in match editors still reads the global catalogue.

## Import teams from league directories (global administrators)

Open **Administration → Team catalogue** (`/en/dashboard/teams`) and choose
the game tab. The **Wardogs** tab offers **Scrape teams from Wardogs League**, using
[the full Wardogs League teams page](https://wardogsleague.net/teams?all=1).
The **Hell Let Loose** tab offers **Sync teams from HeLO (2024)**, using the
public data behind [HeLO's 2024 team directory](https://helo-system.de/teams?series=2024).

The button adds missing teams to the **global catalogue for that game**, with
their name, short code (HeLO calls this the tag), and a stored copy of the logo.
It does not register those teams in the competition automatically; use the
competition's team picker afterwards.

Matching uses the normalized name or short code within the selected game.
A matching active team keeps its identity, name, code, links and workspace
association, while its logo is refreshed from the provider. This also repairs
teams imported earlier without logos. Archived and merged entries are left
unchanged; ambiguous matches are never used to overwrite a team. Unchanged
logo bytes reuse the stored asset without creating a new revision.

The result shows created teams, updated logos, skipped teams and failures.
Missing source logos never clear a saved logo. Failed downloads leave existing
teams unchanged and are reported for retry; new teams with a failed logo are
not created. Imported GIFs use a static first frame. The Wardogs importer selects
`/api/logos/...` images, and HeLO uses its API's `icon` or rendered **Team Icon**
image. Source reads and downloads are bounded and restricted to provider hosts.

Run the import again to repair existing catalogue logos. Saved match snapshots
remain historical: use **Refresh snapshot** on an existing match to adopt a
new catalogue logo. Upcoming and recent Discord cards are redrawn by the bot
when that snapshot changes.
This import is available only to Logi global administrators.

## Register teams in a competition (global administrators)

Open **Administration → Competitions → Manage → Teams**. Choose a division,
open **Choose teams**, and tick as many catalogue teams as you need. Search
matches names and short codes; selected teams stay selected while you search.
**Select all matching** selects the matching unregistered teams across all
catalogue pages, up to the competition's remaining capacity (500 teams total).
Use **Clear selection** or remove an individual selected chip to adjust the
selection, then select **Done** and **Register … teams** once.

The complete batch is validated before any registrations are added. An archived,
merged, missing or other-game team rejects the batch; your selection remains
available to correct and retry. Teams already registered by another
administrator or by a retried submission are skipped without moving them to a
different division. The same catalogue team may be registered in several
competitions; each competition keeps its own fixtures and results.

## Participating in competitions

Clan administrators enroll their linked teams from **Competitions** in the clan
sidebar. Registration can be closed, automatic or subject to Logi administrator
approval. A competition can require a minimum number of clan members who have
signed into Logi; members do not need verified Steam accounts or to use the
other clan tools. Withdrawal preserves the team's competition history. See
[match and competition guidance](/wiki/public/matches-and-competitions).

## Competitions-only workspace

Administrators opening a new, unconfigured clan are asked whether to use the
full Logi toolset or **Competitions only**. The latter keeps the sidebar focused
on the dashboard, competitions, the team profile, players and settings. It changes
navigation rather than permissions, stops no integrations and deletes no data.
You can change the clan-wide preference at any time in **Clan settings →
Workspace mode**, including when an existing clan wants the simpler menu.
