# Events

Events are the central schedule for a server. They can represent a general community event, a match, or another planned activity. Managers create and edit events; members use them to understand timing and respond when sign-up is enabled.

## Create an event (manager)

<Steps>

### Start a new event

Open the workspace **Events** page and choose **Create event**. Enter a descriptive name before adding optional details.

### Set the schedule

Add the meeting time and, where needed, the game start/end time. These are distinct: meeting time is when people gather; game time is when the activity is expected to run.

### Add operational details

Choose available map, side, mode, capacity, topic preset, stratmaps, and Discord-related options only when they apply to the event.

### Save and verify

After saving, reopen the event and confirm the time, timezone, sign-up settings, and visibility are correct before telling people about it.

</Steps>

## Sign-ups and attendance

<Tabs items={["Player", "Manager"]}>
    <Tabs.Tab>
        When an event accepts sign-ups, choose the response that honestly
        describes your availability. A sign-up is not always the same as a final
        roster assignment; managers can still organise people into squads later.
    </Tabs.Tab>
    <Tabs.Tab>
        Configure the allowed sign-up statuses deliberately. Review responses
        before building a roster, especially people who declined or have not
        responded. Do not assume every visible member is available.
    </Tabs.Tab>
</Tabs>

## Event status and conclusion

An event progresses through its scheduled lifecycle. Managers can conclude an event when it is finished and record relevant results. A completed-looking date does not automatically mean that the operational outcome has been entered.

<Callout type="warning">
    Changing core scheduling or capacity information after people have signed up
    can confuse attendees. Check the event’s connected roster and Discord
    communication before making late changes.
</Callout>
