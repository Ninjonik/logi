# Matches

A match is a competitive event with additional match details. Members can use the list to find upcoming or previous matches. Managers can create, edit, prepare and conclude matches; Logi's global administrators link matches to competition fixtures.

## The match list

**Matches** lists matches and trainings together, soonest first, grouped by
week in the clan's time zone (**This week**, **Next week**, then **Week of …**).
Each row shows the date and start time, the name and category, the game, map
and side (and the competition, when the match is linked to a fixture of a
published competition), how many players signed up and the event's phase:

- **Sign-ups until …** while registration is open (the weekday within the
  next six days, otherwise the date);
- **Roster missing** or **Roster · draft** once registration has closed and the
  roster is not published yet;
- **Roster published**, or how many rostered players have not confirmed;
- after play, the score with **Result awaiting confirmation**, **Victory ·
  confirmed** (or defeat, draw, corrected), **No result yet**, or for a
  training how many passed and failed.

**Upcoming** shows what is still ahead with up to three events played in the
last two weeks below it; **Played** shows past events, newest first, thirty at
a time with **Show more**. Filter by **Matches** and **Trainings**, by game, or
search by name, opponent or map; **Clear filters** brings everything back.

### Drafts (manager)

A match saved with **Save as draft** in the new-match flow, or autosaved while
you fill it in, is a draft: the bot does not announce it and members never see
it. Drafts appear only in the
**Drafts** tab (with their number, for example **Drafts · 1**), never under
Upcoming or Played. Each shows **not announced**, the **Draft** label and, when
no opponent is chosen yet, **no opponent yet**. Open a draft to continue in the
new-match form and publish it from there.

### Waiting for you (manager)

At the top, **Waiting for you** lists up to three tasks of each kind:

- **Publish the roster** for matches whose sign-ups have closed, soonest
  first, with the start and the open slots;
- **Confirm the result** for played matches whose imported, collected or
  entered result has not been confirmed yet, with its source and score;
- **Check attendance** for matches played in the last two weeks whose roster
  was published but whose attendance has not been closed yet, with the number
  of rostered players who did not confirm.

Each task opens the matching tab of the match. The result state is read fresh
on every visit, so a confirmed result leaves the list straight away. A manager
also lands on the roster or result tab when opening a row that needs one.

## Recurring matches

**Recurring** lists match series in the same rows as the match list; each row
starts with its schedule, for example **repeats every Wednesday**. A match
created with **Repeat every week** is a weekly series: Logi creates the next
matches of the series on its own, two weeks ahead, at the same local time in
the clan's time zone, with the same teams, map, sign-up and Discord settings
and empty sign-ups. Each one is announced like any other match. The bot creates
them, so they appear only while the Logi bot is running. To change or stop a
series, open its first match with **Edit series** and change or switch off the
repeat settings, then save; matches already created stay as they are. Monthly
repeat settings are only labels for now: Logi does not create monthly matches.

## Create a match (manager)

Select **Matches** in the active workspace and choose **New match** (or **New
training** under Trainings). Creating takes five steps; the step pills at the
top show where you are, and you can jump to any step. Beside the steps,
**Preview in Discord** shows the registration announcement as players will see
it, in the clan's bot language, and outlines the part the current step changes.
Discord shows the times in each reader's own time zone; the message never shows
the server password.

<Steps>

### Match

Pick the **game** and a **template** (see
[match templates](/wiki/configuration/presets-and-templates#match-templates)).
The template fills the times, sign-ups and Discord settings of the next steps.
A training template turns the flow into a training: no opponent and no map.
Under **Teams**, your clan is listed with its side; choose the **opponent**
from the global team catalogue and its side. A team missing from the catalogue
can be requested with **Request it**; it becomes selectable once Logi's
administrators approve it. Choose the **map**, **time of day** and, for Hell
Let Loose, the **middle point**. The **name** is filled in from the teams and
the template ("VLK vs ROG · Friendly") until you change it.

### Time

Enter the start date and time. The box below shows the timeline the template
gives: announcement, registration close, meeting and end. **Change for this
match only** lets you adjust those offsets without touching the template.
Switch on **Repeat every week** for a weekly series.

### Sign-ups

Choose which member statuses may sign up. The offered groups and their caps
come from the template: when a capped group is full, a player who picks it is
signed up as a reserve without a group instead, and the bot tells them so.
The sign-up reminder row shows who gets the daily reminder.

### Discord

Choose the announcement channel and the roster channel (the clan defaults are
preselected), whom the announcement pings, whether the match forum and the
squad voice channels are created, and the server name and password. Only
players on the published roster ever see the password.

### Review

Check the summary; **Edit** goes back to a step. **Publish** creates the match:
the bot posts the announcement (straight away, or when the template's
announcement time is reached) and creates the forum. You build the roster after
registration closes; it starts from the template's squad preset.

</Steps>

### Drafts

Work in progress is saved as a **draft** a moment after each change ("Draft
saved at …" next to the title), and **Save as draft** in the review step saves
it on purpose. A draft is visible only to the clan's managers in the
dashboard: the bot does not announce it, nobody can sign up, and it is left out
of calendars, public pages, the website feed and `/api/v1`. Open a draft from
the match list to continue where you stopped (the address keeps
`?draftId=…`). **Publish** turns it into the match; **Delete draft** removes it
after a confirmation. Only drafts can be deleted this way.

### Record the outcome

After play, enter the result and conclude the event when your workflow is complete.

</Steps>

## The match page

Opening a match shows its name, game, map and time (meeting and start, or when
it was played), **View in Discord** once the bot has posted the sign-up
announcement (until the match is played) and, for managers, a **⋯** menu with
**Edit match**, **Open the roster page** and **Sign-up history**. On a phone
the top shrinks to a back arrow, the match name and the current step ("Step 3
of 5 · Roster"), the Discord tab is left out and the ⋯ menu also holds the
Discord link. Below is the match's progress in five steps:

| Step     | Shows                                                                       |
| -------- | --------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Draft    | When the match was created, or when its sign-ups open                       |
| Sign-ups | Until when people can sign up, when sign-ups closed, then how many signed up |
| Roster   | Not created yet, a draft to publish before the meeting, or the player count |
| Match    | The meeting time, then how many players attended                            |
| Result   | Import from the server, awaiting confirmation, confirmed or corrected       |

The current step is highlighted; earlier steps are ticked. A match counts as
closed 15 minutes after its game end even before Logi records it, and a match
with a confirmed or corrected result has every step done. Members see the
result step without the review state, which only managers can read.

The page has five tabs. Before the match the sign-ups tab is called
**Sign-ups** with the number of players signed up; after it, **Sign-ups and
attendance**.

- **Overview**: the match form (details, schedule, Discord options, teams).
- **Sign-ups and attendance**: see below.
- **Roster**: the [roster board](/wiki/operations/rosters), or a prompt to
  create the roster. Members see only a published roster.
- **Discord**: which messages the bot posted for the match (sign-up
  announcement, match info, match thread, roster update and the Discord event
  with its state), each with a link into Discord, and the channels in use.
- **Result**: the result review (managers) and a link to the imported match
  statistics.

## Sign-ups and attendance (manager)

The **Sign-ups and attendance** tab counts roster players who attended, were
excused or did not attend, reserves who attended, people who declined and
members who never answered. The table lists the roster players in squad order,
then the reserves, with their place, what they said before the match, their
attendance and the points it gives under the workspace's
[membership score rules](/wiki/configuration/members). Filters switch to the
people who declined or did not answer; **Sign-up history** opens the event's
sign-up activity.

- **Attended** records manager-confirmed attendance on the roster, the same
  status as **Confirmed** on the roster board.
- **Before the match** shows **Confirmed attendance**, **Not confirmed**,
  **Reserve**, or **Running late** with the text a player sent through the
  **Running late** button in Discord.
- **Excused** can be set by a manager for any roster player or reserve; it
  scores like a running-late notice (the **Excused absence** rule). A player
  who sent a running-late notice stays at least excused, so **Did not attend**
  is not available for them. Without a notice or an excuse, a player who did
  not come is **Did not attend**.
- Changes show as **Unsaved changes: N** until you **Save attendance**.
- **Load from voice channel** marks roster players and reserves who are in the
  meeting voice channel as attended (see
  [rosters](/wiki/operations/rosters#attendance-and-late-changes)). Next to the
  button Logi says when it was last read, for example "Last read at 20:05: 19
  players in Sraz", and the table marks players it found with **from the voice
  channel**.
- The **No response** filter lists members who never answered the sign-up.
  While sign-ups are open, **Remind** sends them the sign-up reminder by
  Discord DM.

Attendance can change until the match is closed. **Close match and award
points** first shows how many players each rule
covers and the points it gives, for example "19 attended from the roster +3",
and how many players' points change. Save attendance first: closing is not
available with unsaved changes. Closing cannot be undone. A match nobody closes
closes itself 15 minutes after its end, with the same points.

## Teams

Hell Let Loose and Wardogs matches have a **Teams** section next to the map and
side details. It uses the Logi [team catalogue](/wiki/operations/teams), which
Logi's global administrators maintain for every workspace. Hell Let Loose:
Vietnam matches and trainings have no Teams section.

- **Hell Let Loose:** up to two teams (Team A and Team B), each with an
  optional side: **Allies** or **Axis**.
- **Wardogs:** up to three teams (Team A, Team B and Team C), each with an
  optional faction: **Valkyra**, **Manticore** or **Lonestar**.

Each slot has a searchable picker that lists the game's active catalogue teams
with logo, name and short code. Open it with the keyboard or mouse, type to
search, and choose with the arrow keys and Enter. **No team** empties the slot.
**No side** leaves the side open.

When the team you need is missing, the last picker entry offers a request
instead: **Request “Name”** with the name you typed, or **Request a new team**
when nothing is typed or the typed name is already listed. It opens the
[team request form](/wiki/operations/teams#request-a-new-team) prefilled with
that name; the rest of the unsaved match form is kept. A requested team is not
selected automatically: once a Logi administrator approves it (the requester
gets a Discord direct message), it appears in the picker and can be selected
like any other team. Until then, the match can be saved with the slot empty.

A team can occupy only one slot, and a side can be given to only one team; the
editor marks such conflicts before saving. A match can be saved with empty or
partly filled slots while the opponent is unknown, and clearing every slot then
saving removes the assignments. Choosing teams does not change the match's own
**Side** field, its roster, attendance or results.

### Saved team details

Saving a newly selected team stores a snapshot of its name, short code and
logo on the match. Later catalogue renames, logo changes, archiving and merging
do not change saved matches. Moving a team to another slot or changing its side keeps
its snapshot; replacing it with a different team captures that team's details.
If a newly selected team is archived before you save, the save is rejected with
a message so you can choose another team.

A saved team that has since been archived stays on the match with an
**Archived** badge until you replace it; archived teams cannot be newly
selected. To pick up catalogue changes for a saved team, use **Refresh
snapshot** on a saved match. It works only while the team is active in the
catalogue and the match is not concluded, and it updates the stored snapshot
immediately without saving other changes in the form.

When Logi administrators merge a duplicate into another team, a saved team that
was merged shows a **Merged** badge. **Refresh snapshot** then switches that
slot to the surviving team and captures its details, keeping the slot and side.

Once a match is concluded, its teams and snapshots are frozen: the Teams
section becomes read-only and saving other match fields leaves them unchanged.
A match counts as concluded from 15 minutes after its game end, even before
Logi records the conclusion.
Result corrections continue through the reviewed-result workflow below.

## Competition links

Competitions are run by Logi's global administrators, who link a match to a
competition fixture from the competition's management page. The match must be
for the competition's game and not already linked to another fixture. When
teams are assigned to the match, both fixture teams must be among them, so
assign the correct catalogue teams. Workspace managers cannot link matches
themselves. Linking a match does not replace the organiser’s official
competition rules or results process.

<Callout type="info">
    If your match should count for a competition, assign both teams and ask a
    Logi global administrator to link it. Results imported for a linked match
    update the fixture's score.
</Callout>

## Teams on Discord match cards

When teams from the team catalogue are assigned to a match, its
Discord messages show a **Teams** line next to the side, listing each team as
name, short code and side in slot order (for example
`Alpha [ALP] (Allies) vs Bravo (Axis)`). The event information card also shows
the logo of each assigned team that has one: up to two teams for Hell Let Loose
and three for Wardogs. Teams without a logo appear only as text. Labels and
logos come from the snapshot saved when the team was assigned; later catalogue
edits change them only if that snapshot is refreshed before the match concludes.
Matches without assigned teams look exactly as before, and the cards keep their
room, sign-up buttons, map and banner.

## Result, conclusion, and scoring are separate

Recording a match result stores the result and can update a linked competition
fixture. Concluding the match changes its lifecycle, removes its Discord event
messages, completes or cancels its linked Discord scheduled event, and triggers
the one-time score calculation. The calculation uses the workspace score
settings, roster position, manager-confirmed attendance, decline status, and
absence notices; it does not infer attendance from merely signing up or
acknowledging a reminder.

After an imported scoreboard is saved, Logi refreshes workspace and player
performance trends in background jobs. The saved match and conclusion do not
wait for those derived charts, so a performance chart can update shortly after
the match result appears.

When a recorded result includes linked roster-player statistics, opted-in
rostered players with an explicitly linked Discord account are eligible for a
personal match recap by Discord DM. An imported player ID alone is not a Discord
recipient. The recap uses
the workspace's configured clan language and opens the matching localized
public-statistics page. Its comparison uses the same previously recorded player
matches shown on that page.

Turning recaps off blocks pending delivery while that preference is disabled.
An account link that no longer matches the queued recipient also blocks delivery;
old queued recaps without a recorded recipient are withheld. These are best-effort
notifications: a message already in flight may arrive after opting out, and a
failure between sending and recording success can cause a duplicate.

## Review a result (manager)

Open the match's **Result** tab. The score board puts your clan's side first
(found from the side the match was played on and the teams picked for it), with
the team names and logos, the two scores and **Win**, **Loss** or **Draw**.
**Where the result comes from** names the
collected HLL server game the scores came from. Before anything is saved, Logi
suggests the game that started closest to the match start (within three hours)
and fills in its scores; **Other game** picks a different game, an existing
imported result, or manual entry. Selecting a source does not approve it.
**Save without confirming** stores a provisional version; **Confirm result**
saves any unsaved edits as a provisional version and then confirms it. An empty
score is unknown; zero is a known score. The **History** column lists each
saved version with its time and who saved or reviewed it.

The form supports two to sixteen participants, including manual Wardogs factions.
Current Wardogs collection supplies server status, not match history. HLL source
candidates are the fifty most recently fetched sessions. A changed or incomplete
source cannot be confirmed; refresh, select the current source and review again.

After confirmation, enter a reason and use **Save correction**. The previous
version remains in the recent revision history with its reviewer and timestamp.
A later import does not silently replace a reviewed result. Another manager's
concurrent save requires you to refresh before saving your version.

Player attribution uses active verified Steam links at review time. Manually
entered IDs and player names do not verify an account. Unlinking affects future
reviews; it does not rewrite past review history. Counts do not establish team
membership, attendance or game ownership.

**After confirmation** lists what confirming does:

- the result appears in the channel of the workspace's Discord results panel,
  when one is set up for the game (otherwise the page says it stays in Logi and
  the clan API);
- players get their personal summary by DM, which today is already sent when
  the statistics are imported;
- for a match linked to a competition fixture, a confirmed or corrected HLL
  result fills the fixture score, the same way an imported result does: each
  fixture team takes the score of the Axis or Allies side it played. Without
  both sides known the fixture is left to the competition administrators.

Reviewed results are separate from the legacy imported scoreboard and event
conclusion described above. A confirmed result does not automatically publish
anything on an external website.
