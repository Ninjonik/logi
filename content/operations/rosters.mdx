# Rosters

Rosters turn an event’s available people into squads and assigned positions. They are powerful planning tools, so make changes deliberately and publish only when the arrangement is ready to share.

## Build a roster (manager)

<Steps>

### Start from the event context

Create a roster from the workspace Roster page and associate it with the appropriate event. Check the event’s sign-ups and attendance information first.

### Choose a layout or template

Add squads and slots manually, or use a squad preset when the standard structure fits. Changing a template later can move or remove layout details, so review the confirmation.

### Place people thoughtfully

Drag a person into a slot, add them to reserves, or mark them not attending. A person can only hold one active place in the roster structure at a time.

### Save as draft, then publish

Save while planning. Publish only when you are ready for people to rely on the assignments. The publish flow can offer notifications and roster-change posting options.

</Steps>

## Three roster lists

<Tabs items={["Assigned", "Reserve", "Not attending"]}>
    <Tabs.Tab>
        Assigned players occupy a squad slot. They are the planned line-up.
    </Tabs.Tab>
    <Tabs.Tab>
        Reserves are available but not currently in a squad slot. Their order
        can be used for prioritisation.
    </Tabs.Tab>
    <Tabs.Tab>
        Not attending records people who declined or should not be assigned. It
        prevents accidental assignment while keeping the status visible to
        managers.
    </Tabs.Tab>
</Tabs>

## Attendance and late changes

Managers can update attendance status and, where available, confirm attendance from the configured meeting channel. Treat that as evidence for the roster, not as an automatic replacement for human review. When changing a published roster, choose notification options carefully so people receive only the communication they need.

<Callout type="warning">
    Deleting a draft roster is destructive. Confirm that it has not replaced or
    been confused with the roster you intend to keep. Published roster changes
    can affect attendees immediately.
</Callout>
