# Rosters

Rosters turn an event’s available people into squads and assigned positions. They are powerful planning tools, so make changes deliberately and publish only when the arrangement is ready to share.

Connected websites may read published squads, reserves and native attendance
through the separate `roster-summaries` grant. Editing remains in Logi/Discord.
Drafts, custom guest names, notes and absence reasons are excluded. Unpublishing
removes the shared projection. An acknowledgement or attendance confirmation
does not prove that a player completed a game; websites must preserve that
distinction and apply their own explicit profile publication consent.

## Build a roster (manager)

<Steps>

### Start from the event context

Create a roster from the workspace Roster page and associate it with the appropriate event. Check the event’s sign-ups and attendance information first.

### Choose a layout or template

Add squads and slots manually, or use a squad preset when the standard structure fits. Changing a template later can move or remove layout details, so review the confirmation.

### Place people thoughtfully

Drag a person into a slot, add them to reserves, or mark them not attending. A person can only hold one active place in the roster structure at a time.

### Save as draft, then publish

Save while planning. Publish only when you are ready for people to rely on the assignments. **Publish roster** opens the publish dialog, which shows exactly what the bot posts; see [publish to Discord](#publish-to-discord).

Saving requires a current Logi login and current workspace management access.
If either changes while the board is open, sign in again or ask a workspace
administrator before retrying. A roster remains attached to its original event;
templates must belong to the same workspace.

</Steps>

There is one persisted roster per event. Saving a roster reconciles it against
the event: newly tracked attendees become reserves if they are not already in a
squad, while tracked non-attendees go to **not attending**. People who are no
longer tracked are removed. Existing squad placements are preserved during a
normal roster save, but a membership or sign-up change can still refresh an
open match roster—review it after those changes.

Rosters use the same game scope as their event, and a squad preset can only be
applied when it belongs to that game. Legacy records without a game are treated
as Hell Let Loose. Automatic member lists use only memberships for that event's
game; belonging to HLL alone does not add someone to a Wardogs roster. Explicit
event sign-ups and manually placed guests keep their existing behavior.

When starting a new roster, Logi tracks people who had an assignment on or
before the earlier of the current time and the event's registration end, as well
as anyone already in its participant or sign-up lists. An assignment created
after registration closed is therefore not automatically added to that roster's
starting pool. The initial reserve and not-attending lists are sorted by the
workspace score, then name; that order is a starting point, not an automatic
team-selection decision.

## The roster board

The roster opens on the match page under **Roster**, and on its own roster page.
Managers switch the board between three modes at the top: **View only**,
**Squad editor** (squads and slots) and **Roster editor** (who plays where).
Next to them are the squad preset, **Confirm from meeting VC** with the name of
the meeting voice channel (for example "· Sraz 1") and, in the roster editor,
**Auto-fill**. On phones the preset, the voice-channel confirmation, auto-fill
and deleting or unpublishing sit behind the **⋯** button next to the modes.

- **Unsaved changes: N** counts the edits since the last save: each changed
  slot or squad, and each player moved in or out of the reserves or the
  not-attending list. Save keeps the roster a draft; **Publish roster** asks
  first. Deleting a draft is the bin icon and also asks first.
- The line under the toolbar explains the status icons next to each name: a
  double tick for confirmed by a manager, a tick for confirmed by the player,
  and a clock for not confirmed yet. Managers change the status by clicking the
  icon; in the roster editor the same menu moves the player to the reserves or
  to not attending.
- Each player shows their Discord avatar, group icons, score and K/D, and on
  the right their group. In the roster editor the ⓧ button takes a player out
  of the slot and back to the reserves.
- The reserves and not-attending lists show how many people they hold. Under
  the reserves, **No response: N members** counts members of the match's game
  who have not answered the sign-up; **Remind** sends them the sign-up
  reminder by Discord DM while sign-ups are open. A reminder to the same
  people of a match can be sent once every 30 minutes.
- In the roster editor, drag a player from the reserves into a slot; the free
  slot under the pointer says **Drop here: name**. Or tap a free slot (**Pick
  player**) and choose from the list. Tapping works on phones, where dragging
  does not.
- On phones each squad folds to its header; tap it to open or close it. The
  first squad starts open. **Publish roster** (and **Save** while there are
  unsaved changes) stays at the bottom of the screen.

## Attendance and late changes

Each assigned player and reserve has one of three attendance states:

| State        | Meaning                                                               | How it is set                               |
| ------------ | --------------------------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------- |
| Pending      | No response recorded                                                  | Default, or a manager resets it             |
| Acknowledged | The player used Logi's Discord attendance button, or a manager set it | This is not proof of physical attendance    |
| Confirmed    | Manager-recorded attendance                                           | Used as present attendance in event scoring |

The Discord acknowledgement button works only while the event is **starting**,
the roster is published, and the person is assigned to a squad or reserve. It
cannot add someone to the roster. Managers can update attendance status
directly for a rostered player or reserve.

Managers can also use **Confirm from meeting VC** in the roster's view and
roster editor modes, or **Load from voice channel** under **Sign-ups and
attendance** on the match page. It checks only the
people already assigned to a squad or reserve and marks those currently in the
configured meeting voice/stage channel as confirmed. It does not inspect other
voice channels, move people, add anyone to the roster, or prove that a person
stayed for the event. The action asks the running bot for the current members
of that one channel; it does not query every rostered person or track voice
changes in Logi. The action needs a configured meeting channel and a running
bot with access to read that channel; it is available to managers even if the
roster is not published.

A published roster is visible to non-managers; a draft is not.

## Publish to Discord

**Publish roster** (and **Publish again** for a roster that is already
published) opens one dialog. On the left you choose; on the right a preview
shows the message as it will look in the roster channel.

- **What goes to Discord:** **Photo and text roster** (the default) posts the
  roster image with every squad written out under it, so it can be searched
  and read on a phone. **Photo only** posts just the image, like the old bot.
  The choice is made for each publish; the default comes from **Messages and
  panels → Roster**.
- **Channel:** the event-information channel from **Channels and language**,
  shown read-only. Without a separate channel the match announcement carries
  the roster image instead.
- **Mention rostered players:** on the first publish the message mentions
  everyone in a squad slot. Off by default when publishing again, because the
  change DMs already reach the people concerned.
- **Counters:** players in the roster, reserves and not attending.

Publishing again edits the same Discord message; no new one is posted. The
dialog lists the **changes since the last version** (who is new, who left or
went to the reserves, who moved squad or changed role) and offers:

- **Tell players about changes by DM:** only players whose place changed get a
  DM in the clan language with their new squad and role.
- **Post the change summary to the channel:** a short message under the
  roster. There is one summary per match; each later publish edits it and adds
  the new changes.

Both default to the choice in **Messages and panels → Roster**. The bot sends
the DMs and the summary a few seconds after the save. If some players have
closed DMs, Logi says so after publishing ("The DM did not reach 2 players:
Mrak, Ježek."); the roster is saved either way.

The DMs, the summary and these per-publish choices are dashboard actions. The
clan API (`/api/v1`) publishes a roster with the clan's defaults and without
mentions, DMs or a summary; the defaults themselves are the `matchMessages`
settings slice.

### The roster message

The message title is `Roster · Name · Category · day date · time`, followed by
the map, the meeting time and channel and the filled slots ("18 of 22 places").
With the text roster, squads are grouped under the clan's root groups, one line
per squad with short role names ("F1 · SL Rex_CZ · Medic Medvěd · MG Bizon ·
Rifleman —", where "—" is an open slot), then the reserves and the players not
attending. A roster too long for Discord's 4000-character limit falls back to the
image with **Show full roster**. The footer says when it was published.

Buttons:

- **My assignment** (private): your squad, role, squad leader, the leader's
  note, the meeting, and the server with its password. From the day before the
  meeting it offers **Confirm attendance**; until the game starts **Running
  late**.
  Reserves see their reserve card; people not on the roster are told so.
- **Show roster** (private): one squad at a time with every role, player and
  whether they confirmed, are pending or are coming late; a menu switches to
  another squad or the reserves.
- **Open roster:** the public roster page.

### Match forum

When the match has a forum, the bot keeps a **Match information** post (sides,
start, map, meeting channel, the server name without its password, notes and
named stratmap links, **My assignment**), one post per topic of the topic
preset, and a pinned **Debrief** post. Once the result is confirmed, Debrief
shows it with **View match**. If **Messages and panels** turns on attendance
notices, a running-late or can't-make-it notice is posted in the match
information thread with the player's name and arrival time; the reason stays in
Logi for the organisers.

<Callout type="warning">
    Only drafts can be deleted. You must unpublish a published roster before
    deleting it. Published roster changes can affect attendees immediately.
</Callout>
