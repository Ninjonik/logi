# Trainings

Trainings are practice-focused scheduled sessions. The page keeps them separate from competitive matches so communities can plan both without mixing their purpose or completion flow.

<Steps>

### Create a training (manager)

Open **Trainings**, select the create action, and give the session a useful name.

### Set an accurate start and end

Set the meeting time and the session’s planned timing. An end time helps members understand the commitment and helps managers plan subsequent activity.

### Share the plan

Add the relevant details and use the server’s normal communication process so people know what the training is for.

### Complete it afterwards

When the training has taken place, use the completion action if your role permits it. Logi requires a completion result for every attendee before it will conclude the training. This keeps the list and history accurate.

</Steps>

<Callout type="warning">
    Do not mark a training complete just because its scheduled time passed. Only
    complete it once the community’s operational record is ready.
</Callout>

## Training rules that differ from matches

Trainings use a simple **Attend** or **Not attending** response; they do not
offer groups, general signup, match membership-status restrictions, or forum
posts. A training can remain open for sign-up after its status becomes
**starting**, right up to its registration deadline. Its event status still
concludes automatically at game end during status reconciliation, so record
completion results before then when attendance history matters.
