# Tickets and membership applications

This page configures two Discord workflows for the selected workspace: support
tickets and membership applications. It is not a public help desk by itself.
The bot creates and maintains the Discord panels after the configuration is
complete. Configure them in **Settings → Tickets** and **Settings → Membership**.

**Settings → Tickets** shows the on switch, the four steps of a ticket, the
panel (channel, thread channel, heading, text, optional image and the panel's
own colour) beside a preview of the exact Discord card the bot posts, and the
categories as a table: who answers and how many questions each has. **Edit**
opens a category's button text, emoji, description, the title of its thread
card, support roles and form questions. **Settings → Membership** is the
[clan application](/wiki/configuration/membership-applications): one form in
several Discord windows, the decision buttons and the optional web form.
Both pages save with the bar at the bottom, which only saves that page;
**Discard** drops unsaved changes.

## Required configuration

Both workflows require the bot to be running and the selected submit channel to
be a normal Discord text channel. An incomplete configuration is simply
skipped: no panel is posted and no partial fallback panel is created.

| Workflow                | Required settings                                                              | What each category adds                                                                                                                       |
| ----------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------ | --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Support tickets         | Enabled, submit channel, ticket parent channel, and at least one category      | Label/emoji, optional description, thread card title, support roles, up to five questions                                                     |
| Membership applications | Enabled, submit channel, application parent channel, and at least one category | Assignment type, support roles, recruit/final roles; the questions are in the [application form](/wiki/configuration/membership-applications) |

The bot posts or updates one panel message in each configured submit channel.
If its saved panel message is deleted, the next successful sync creates a new
one. Test after changing channels so staff do not leave an old panel in use.

<Callout type="warning">
    The parent channel must permit the bot to create private threads and manage
    their members. Support staff need a configured support role, the dashboard
    admin role, or Discord's Administrator permission to close a tracked thread.
    Giving a role visibility of a parent channel alone does not make it a Logi
    support role.
</Callout>

## What happens when someone uses a panel

### Support ticket

The panel is one card in the clan colour, or in the colour set under **Panel
colour** (leave it empty for the clan colour). It shows the heading, the text,
one line per category ("**Report a player** · behaviour on the server") and one
grey button per category, all equally important, with **Managed in Logi** at
the bottom. With more than ten categories the buttons become one select. The
card follows the clan language and has no "Automatic updates" button.

1. The member chooses a category. If the category has questions, Discord shows
   its window with the category's name, questions and placeholders; otherwise
   Logi creates the ticket immediately.
2. The bot creates a private thread under the configured ticket parent channel,
   assigns it a sequential workspace ticket number, and adds the requester plus
   configured category support members where Discord permissions allow.
   Dashboard administrators are not automatically added. The thread is named
   after the category and number, for example **Report a player #12**.
3. The thread opens with a card: "TICKET #12 · REPORT A PLAYER", the
   category's **thread card title** (for example
   `{author} is reporting a player`; empty reads "Player 17 · Report a
   player"), the **Open** chip with the time, the questions with their answers and "Support closes the ticket
   with /close_ticket". Above the card the bot mentions only the author and
   the category's support roles; nobody else is pinged.
4. The member gets a private "Ticket #12 is open" with **Open thread**. When
   tickets are switched off they read "Tickets are switched off right now".
   If the thread cannot be created or stored, the member reads only "The
   ticket couldn't be opened. Nothing was saved. The admins have been
   notified" and the cause (for example a missing bot permission) goes to the
   errors channel.

Staff close the tracked thread with `/close_ticket`, optionally with a reason.
Logi records who closed it and when, posts the close card in the thread
("Resolved", the **Closed** chip, who closed it and when, the reason as a
quote), DMs the author a card with the category, who closed it, the quoted
reason and **Open thread**, renames the thread to **closed · Report a player
#12**, then locks and archives it. Without a reason the quote is left out.
The private reply says whether the author got the DM ("The author can't
receive DMs; they'll find the summary in the thread"); a failed DM never
stops the ticket from closing and is not posted to the errors channel.

Before closing, the bot reloads the server, its role definitions and the
member, the same check as `/close_application`. If Discord cannot confirm
them, it leaves the ticket open ("Your roles can't be checked right now");
previously cached administrator access does not authorize closure. Someone
outside the category's support roles reads who closes that category's
tickets. Used outside a ticket thread, in a thread that is not a ticket or on
a closed ticket, the command says so in the clan language.

### Player reports

Support categories can also receive **Report Player** tickets from a configured
CRCON or Warcon public panel. Select the category with **Report player** in the
live server panel's editor (**Discord → Discord panels**);
the selector shows the private-thread parent configured here. The player chooses
an observed player or enters a name, then supplies a reason and optional evidence.
The interaction and resulting thread are private to the reporter and designated
staff. Names entered manually are unverified; reports never trigger automatic bans.
Staff close these reports with `/close_ticket`. Reports have one-minute cooldowns,
a three-active-report limit and durable recovery after a bot restart. Failed or
uncertain creation is reconciled before another thread can be created.

Private reports require complete, current permission checks. Unrelated people
with Manage Threads can see private threads, so remove that access or designate
the appropriate support staff before enabling this feature. The bot checks the
actual thread members before writing the report. The website API does not export
private reports. See the [implementation and operational limits](https://github.com/Ninjonik/logi/blob/feat/valkyria-integration/docs/integrations/website/hll-live-and-player-reports.md).

### Membership application flow

The application is one form in several Discord windows, sent once after a
review; the private thread is created only then, and recruiters decide with
buttons on the card or with `/close_application`. See
[Clan applications](/wiki/configuration/membership-applications) for the form
builder, the decision buttons, the DMs and the web form.

### Membership role and score settings

**Membership** has two switches at the top: **Applications** publishes the
panel and opens application threads, **Role sync** lets Logi add and remove
membership roles by member status. Role sync follows applications until you
change it, and it also works with applications switched off, for example when
staff assign members on the dashboard only. The Discord panel lists every
category with the description written under it.

The workspace clan role is added for recruit or active membership outcomes.
The category's **recruit roles** apply only to recruit, and its **final roles**
apply only to active member/mercenary outcomes. Pending has no managed roles.
The bot compares actual Discord roles with the authorized desired state and
changes only roles in that game's managed set. Unmanaged roles and another game's
roles are preserved. A category's game decides which game owns its roles;
categories without a game remain Hell Let Loose. Do not reuse one category
role for another game, as a group role, or as the dashboard administrator role.

**Membership role operations** shows pending, in-progress, retry, applied, denied,
superseded and failed requests, their actor/target and recent attempts. Each attempt
checks current staff access, target presence and bot hierarchy. A departure/rejoin
cannot revive an old request. Applied means a fresh check matched the desired roles.
After six consecutive failures, correct the cause and save a new authorized member
request. Importing members does not create role grants. Details and activation
requirements are in the [managed-role handoff](https://github.com/Ninjonik/logi/blob/feat/valkyria-integration/docs/integrations/website/v0.8/README.md).

The bot verifies current Discord permissions before changing roles. A newly granted
dashboard admin role must reach Discord first; old login/access caches cannot authorize
the operation. A player's internal Logi ID is not a Discord link, even when numeric.
Unlinked players remain editable, but their role request is denied without a Discord
call. Changing the linked account invalidates an older queued request; save a new
authorized request after checking the link.

Membership application settings and the Discord panel are shared by the whole
workspace. Each category selects its target game, so one panel can contain
multiple categories for the same game as well as categories for other games.
Configure the shared submit channel, parent channel, roles, and optional
application-thread welcome message once. The welcome message is posted above
the application summary and supports `{applicant}`, `{support_roles}`, and
`{category}` placeholders. Existing categories without a game ID remain Hell
Let Loose categories.

An application, an open application thread, and an existing membership block
only the matching game. For example, an existing Hell Let Loose membership
does not prevent a Wardogs application in the same workspace.

The Discord panel's **Apply** button opens the application windows described
on [Clan applications](/wiki/configuration/membership-applications). Accounts
already linked to the player's Logi profile are prefilled in the window for
game accounts, whichever game they were linked through.

In the dashboard, each category groups its display label, emoji, target game,
and application result before the category description.

Choose whether category support-role members are added to an application thread
individually or invited by the support-role mention in the
opening message. Individual adds are the reliable default. Role mentions let
Discord add eligible small roles, but Discord limits this to roles with fewer
than 100 members and up to 10 mentioned roles. Dashboard administrators can
manage applications without being automatically added to their threads.

**Settings → Membership** also contains the roster-score values used when a concluded
event resolves player scores. They are per-workspace and determine the numeric
result for declined, confirmed/absent roster or reserve, excused absence, and
people with no score category. Change them before the relevant event concludes;
event scores are only resolved once.

## Test the complete path

<Steps>

### Test as a non-staff account

Open each type of request from an account that has no staff role. Check the
questions, category labels, private-thread location, and the exact people who
can see it.

### Test staff closure

Use a configured support role to close the test thread. Then test that an
unrelated member cannot close it. Confirm the close card, the renamed thread
(**closed · …**), the DM card and, with DMs closed on the test account, the
reply that the DM could not be delivered.

### Test a disposable membership role

For an application, use a disposable recruit/final role and a test applicant.
Confirm the chosen outcome produces the expected assignment and role changes
before connecting production roles.

</Steps>

<Callout type="warning">
    Ticket configuration can change how private member requests are routed.
    Review permission and channel choices with a server owner before making
    broad changes.
</Callout>
