# Public Discord panels

Panels are Discord messages the Logi bot keeps up to date and edits in place.
Live panels refresh every 60 seconds. A workspace can have up to 20 panels:

- **Live server**: one panel per game server, with its state, map, players and
  score.
- **Our servers**: an optional extra message listing several servers with a
  join button each. Every server keeps its own panel.
- **Results**: confirmed match results of one game, one card per match. Each
  game has at most one results panel.
- **Calendar**: the clan's upcoming events, the next match highlighted.
- **Competition table**: one table per division of a Logi competition, updated
  after every confirmed result.
- **WD League**: two messages for the whole Wardogs League, the table and the
  next matches with recent results; see [Wardogs League](/configuration/league-tracking).

Panels speak the server's Discord language (English, Czech or German).

All panels are managed on **Settings → Discord → Panely v Discordu** (Discord
panels). Only clan administrators can open it. **Grafika panelů** (Panel
graphics, see [Panel graphics](/configuration/panel-graphics)) and **Seed
serverů** (Server seeding) are pages under it.

## Adding and sending a panel

1. Open **Settings → Discord → Panely v Discordu** and choose **Nový panel**
   (New panel) at the top right.
2. **Typ panelu** (Panel type): choose **Live server**, **Our servers**,
   **Results**, **WD League**, **Calendar** or **Competition table**. A clan
   has at most one results panel per game and one WD League and calendar panel;
   a type the clan already has is greyed out with a note to edit that panel.
   Once a panel has been sent, its type cannot change.
3. Choose the source: the **game server** for a live server (its collection
   state and whether live data works are shown under it), up to ten
   **servers** for Our servers (drag them, or use the arrows, to set their order
   in the message), the **game** for results, the **event categories** for the
   calendar (none means every clan event) or the **competition**. Servers come
   from **Settings → Game servers** (see [Game server data](/configuration/settings#game-server-data)); add one there first.
4. **Kanál** (Channel): pick the panel's channel and choose **Ověřit**
   (Verify). Logi lists the bot permissions the channel has and is missing and
   whether `@everyone` can view it. A private channel says which roles can see
   it; only there can a server's own panel show the server password.
5. **Obsah** (Content): switch on what the message shows. For a live server:
   score, top players, next map, queue, the IP:port (HLL) or join code
   (Wardogs), the **Join** button, the server password, **Show players**,
   **Report player** with its ticket category and the seed progress. The
   address, join code and password are typed in right here; the password is
   stored encrypted and is never shown back. For WD League: the table, the next
   matches, recent results and how many matches to show.
6. **Vzhled** (Look): the panel's name and an optional description, a banner,
   the bar colour (the clan colour or an own colour) and the panel style (as in
   Panel graphics, or A, B or C for this panel only); see [Look](#look).
7. **Obnova** (Refresh): every panel refreshes every 60 seconds. Choose
   whether the message footer says when it was last updated.
8. Check the **preview** on the right. For server panels choose **Načíst data
   ze serveru** (Load data from the server): Logi runs the same live read as the
   bot and redraws the preview from it, with every change you make in the form.
   Live server panels also show the join page a player opens after **Join**.
9. Choose **Uložit** (Save) to keep the panel as **Not sent**, or **Odeslat do
   kanálu** (Send to channel) to save and send it. The bot picks the request up
   within about 15 seconds; the **Doručení** (Delivery) card ticks **Saved**,
   **Bot picked up** and **Sent** (**Uloženo**, **Bot převzal**, **Odesláno**),
   and the page updates itself. **Otevřít
   zprávu v Discordu** (Open message in Discord) then opens the message.

To change a panel later, choose **Upravit** (Edit) on its row. The bar at the
bottom counts the unsaved changes; **Uložit** edits the message in place, no new
message is sent, and a paused panel stays paused. **Zahodit** (Discard) drops
the changes.

The same panel settings can be read and changed through the API
(`GET/PATCH /api/v1/clan/settings`, slice `discordPanels`). Sending,
refreshing, pausing and deleting messages, loading server data, verifying a
channel and the server password stay in the dashboard.

## The panel list

The top of **Panely v Discordu** shows whether the bot is online, its version
and its last contact, and warns when the bot is offline, outdated or not on the
Discord server. **Zdroje dat** (Data sources) shows for every game server
whether Logi collects its data, when the last data came in and whether live
reads work. Below, the panels are grouped: live servers, Our servers, the
**Server control** messages of server seeding (in each server's private admin
channel, with the Seed, Refresh and Pause buttons; **Obnovit teď** redraws
one), results, WD League,
calendar and competitions. Each row shows the channel, whether it is public or
private, when the panel was updated and when the next refresh is due, and the
actions it allows. The page refreshes itself every few seconds.

## Sending, refreshing and pausing

A new panel is saved as **Not sent**. Nothing appears in Discord until you
choose **Send to channel**. The bot picks the request up within about 15
seconds. Every panel shows one state:

- **Published**: the message is in Discord and refreshing.
- **Waiting for the bot**: a request was sent and the bot has not answered yet.
- **Error**: the last attempt failed. Logi names the cause and the fix, for
  example the missing channel permission, and when the next attempt runs. Fix
  it, then choose **Try again**. In the editor, **Delivery** keeps the last
  error even after the panel recovers ("The next attempt 1 min later went
  through").
- **Not sent**: saved, but not in Discord.
- **Paused**: the message stays in Discord with a **Paused** chip and is not
  refreshed until you resume it.

**Refresh now** redraws a panel at once. **Pause** keeps the message and
stops refreshing it; **Resume** starts it again. **Delete message** (in the
editor's bar) takes the message out of Discord and keeps the panel as not
sent. **Remove panel** deletes its messages and its settings. Logi also shows when each
panel was saved, sent and last refreshed, when the next refresh is due, and
whether the bot is online and running a current version. If the bot has not
checked in for three minutes, panels do not refresh until it runs again.

**Load data from the server** runs the same live read as the bot (CRCON for Hell
Let Loose, Warcon for Wardogs) and shows what came back with a preview of the
panel. It never shows keys or the server password.

The bot needs View Channel, Send Messages, Embed Links, Attach Files and Read
Message History in the panel's channel. Without Attach Files the panel has no
image. **Verify** in the editor checks these permissions and whether
`@everyone` can view the channel.

## Live server panel

The panel's state chip is one of **Live** (players on the server), **Empty**
(no players), **Seeding** (a seed is running and the server is below its live
threshold, with a progress bar and a link to the seed call), **Unavailable**
(the server does not answer; the last data time is shown) or **Paused**. A
**New map** chip appears for a short time after the map changes.

The panel shows only what the server reports: map, mode, day or night, players
and capacity, the join queue, time remaining, the score, the next map and the
top players. Hell Let Loose shows the queue, the next map and day or night only
when CRCON reports them. Wardogs shows the three factions with their points and
the players with the most kills and the most money now. If a live read fails,
the panel shows the last collected data instead of disappearing.

In a channel that `@everyone` cannot view, the panel is a clan panel: it also
shows the Logi match running on the server, which clan members are playing
(through verified Steam links) and, if you switch it on, the server password.

### Server password

The server password is stored encrypted. It appears only on a server's own
panel, only with **Show password** switched on, and only while `@everyone`
cannot view the channel. The bot checks this on every refresh. If the channel
becomes public, the bot removes the password from the panel and tells the
admins once in the errors channel: **The server password was removed from the
panel**, with the panel and the channel, why, and how to deny `@everyone`
**View Channel**. The password comes back at the next refresh once the channel
is private again. The password never appears on **Our
servers**, on the join page or in any API response.

### Join a server

**Join** opens Logi's join page for the server, `/join/<server>`. Discord
buttons can only open web links, so the page opens Steam
(`steam://connect/<address>`) immediately. If the browser blocks it, the page
shows the address with **Copy** and the steps for adding the server in Steam.
For Wardogs it shows the join code. The page needs no login and shows only the
server's name, players, map and address or join code.

### Show players

**Show players** answers privately with the current round's players, eight per
page, split by side or faction and sorted by kills. Hell Let Loose lists kills
and deaths; Wardogs lists kills and money now. Use **Previous** and **Next** to
page. The button needs current access to the panel's channel; after the panel
changes, open its current message again.

### Report a player

Select a **Report Player** ticket category to add **Report player** to a public
CRCON or Warcon server panel. The whole flow is private: pick a player from the
current round (or **Another player** for someone who left), describe what
happened and optionally add when and an evidence link. The report becomes a
private thread named **Report #17 · player** where the staff are mentioned once.
Staff close it with `/close_ticket`; reports do not ban anyone automatically.
If a report cannot be sent, the reporter reads why; problems only an admin can
fix are posted to the errors channel. Choose or change the destination in
[Tickets](/configuration/tickets).

## Our servers

The optional **Our servers** message lists up to ten servers in the order you
set, each with its state, map, players and, as switched on, the score, the next
map, the queue, the IP:port of Hell Let Loose servers, the join code of Wardogs
servers and the seed progress while a seed runs. Hell Let Loose servers get a
**Join** button each; Wardogs players join with the code. It shows public data
only: no player names, no password and no clan match.

## Results

Each game has one results panel. Only results confirmed in Logi are posted. A
new results panel first posts the last five confirmed results, then each new
one. A card shows the category, the score row with both sides on one line,
the second side mirrored (`VLK Spojenci ★ 4 : 1 Osa ✚ ROG`, with the faction
emblems), the clan's outcome (**Win**, **Loss** or **Draw**), the
map, the date and who confirmed it. The compact layout shortens the row to
`VLK 4 : 1 ROG`. Wardogs and other multi-team results list the places with
points and the clan's place; a Wardogs League result is labelled with its
fixture number and match type, for example **#38 FRIENDLY**. A correction edits the same card and
says what the score was before. **Match details** links to the public match
page when one exists.

## Calendar and competition tables

The calendar shows the next match with its sign-up deadline, then compact rows
of upcoming events with their type as a word. Titles link to the event's
announcement in Discord. **Open calendar** opens the Logi calendar. You can
limit the calendar to chosen event categories. Times show in each reader's own
time zone.

The calendar's channel is chosen here, in the calendar panel's editor; it is no
longer on **Channels and language**. A clan that set a calendar channel there
before sees that calendar in the list as **Calendar** with **Edit**: the editor
opens a new calendar panel in the same channel, and **Save** takes the existing
message over, so it is edited and not posted again.

A competition table panel posts one table per division with points, wins and
the clan's next match, and links the public competition page.

## Delivery and recovery

The bot retains message IDs across restarts and edits the same message. Changing
channels waits for removal of the old message. If Discord's response to a send
is lost, the bot searches recent messages for an invisible identifier it set on
that message; members never see a marker. **Try again** sends a message whose
delivery Discord never confirmed. Repeating an action never sends a duplicate.

**Map artwork** uses the packaged map or game image as a compact, clickable
thumbnail. The bot uploads it once and reuses the attachment on updates and
restart. The bot installs the faction and status signs as application emoji
itself; workspace administrators do not need to upload faction logos (see
[Panel graphics](/configuration/panel-graphics)). Missing artwork and icons
have readable fallbacks.

## Look

The **Vzhled** (Look) step of the panel editor changes how one panel looks in
Discord. Save the panel to apply it; a sent panel updates within about 15
seconds. The preview shows the change at once.

- **Name** is the message title; without one the panel uses the server's name.
  **Description** adds an optional short sentence under it.
- **Banner** uploads a PNG, JPEG or WebP image of up to 2 MiB and at most
  4096 × 4096 pixels. Logi converts it to WebP of at most 1920 × 1080 pixels and
  shows it above the panel in place of the map image. **Choose uploaded** lists
  the banners already uploaded in this workspace, so several panels can share
  one. Saving waits until a running upload finishes. An upload or choice is
  attached only after you save the panel; **Remove** detaches it on the next
  save. Uploads that are never saved to a panel are removed automatically after
  a day. Banner and team logo uploads share a limit of 10 images every 10
  minutes for each administrator in a workspace. If a banner is no longer
  available to the workspace, saving asks you to upload it again or remove it.
  Discord shows the banner only when Logi runs on a public HTTPS address.
- **Bar colour** is the clan colour, or an own `#RRGGBB` colour for this panel
  only. Stale or paused panels keep the amber warning colour.
- **Panel style** follows **Výchozí styl** in Panel graphics, or sets style A
  (score image), B (banner and thumbnail) or C (compact text) for this panel;
  see [Panel graphics](/configuration/panel-graphics).

Faction and status signs are fixed: the old per-panel faction emoji setting is
gone, and a panel saved in the editor drops an override it had.
