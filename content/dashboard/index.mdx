## The navigation changes with your access

The sidebar shows pages available to your account. Managers can create events and change clan settings; superadmins also have platform tools.

<Callout type="info">
    A missing button is usually an access rule, not a loading problem. First
    confirm the selected workspace, then ask that workspace’s administrator to
    review your access.
</Callout>

### Why a manager page can be missing

Logi distinguishes workspace visibility from management. A person can see a
workspace through membership or dashboard access but still be unable to create
events, change settings, or publish rosters. Manager status comes from an
explicit Logi manager record, a resynced dashboard-admin role, Discord
Administrator, or a stored manager override.

The dashboard-admin role is evaluated from Discord by the bot. It needs the
bot online and able to read members; use the manager resync control after role
changes to update the workspace's manager list. An event/ticket/forum button
can still be missing after manager access is correct if that feature has no
valid Discord configuration.

## Workspace overview

The overview is the starting point for a selected server. When a game is
selected in the sidebar, every block shows only that game.

- **Greeting and date** in the clan's time zone, with the clan's games. Links to
  all matches and, for managers, to a new match.
- **Next match**: the earliest match that has not ended, with its category, map,
  side and meeting time, the teams when they are set, and the roster state
  (no roster, draft or published). With a roster it shows how many places are
  filled. It also counts sign-ups and members who have not answered yet: clan
  members of that game whose membership may sign up, without paused members and
  applicants. That count is left out when the match also requires Discord roles,
  because Logi cannot check them for every member. Managers get a button to
  create or finish the roster; members see the published roster. While sign-ups
  are open, managers can also **remind** the members without an answer: after a
  confirmation the bot sends them a direct message in Discord, and the page
  says how many reminders went out.
- **Waiting for you** (managers only): staged results that wait for
  confirmation, required settings that are still missing, and membership
  applications in the pending phase with the date of the oldest one. Below it
  is the clan setup progress, which opens the clan settings.
- **This week**: today and the next six days with their matches and trainings.
- **Form**: the outcomes of the last ten matches with a known result, oldest
  first. A result with a dashed border still waits for confirmation.
- **Most attendance points this month** (managers only): the four members with
  the most points from this month's closed matches. Points follow the clan's
  score rules under **Settings → Membership → Attendance points**, the same
  rules that change a member's score when a match closes; only members with
  points above zero are listed.
- **Clan statistics**: upcoming events, published rosters, members and presets,
  performance charts over the recorded matches (combat and support
  effectiveness, K/D with kills and deaths, points), and the last ten results
  with the record, win rate and top players.

Apart from the reminder button, the overview does not send reminders: sign-up
reminders go out on the match's own schedule.

When you have a default workspace, the signed-in landing page opens it directly,
but only while you can still open that clan; otherwise you see the list of your
clans. **All clans** at the bottom of the clan switcher (top of the sidebar)
always opens that list, grouped into clans you manage, clans you are a member
of and mercenary clans. The switcher shows five clans first and **Show all**
lists every clan you can open. If Logi needs to resolve a workspace, the
sidebar stays available while the content area shows a placeholder shaped like
the page instead of an empty page. When you have no clan in Logi yet, the
landing page says so and links to the bot setup guide: a clan appears once the
Logi bot is on its Discord server.

Each clan on that list is a tile with its picture, description and number of
members and admins; a tile opens the clan. Managed clans without the bot come
last, marked **Bot missing**. Only clan admins are offered **Invite bot** and
**Refresh** on such a tile. Members and mercenaries see a request to ask a clan
admin instead.

## The sidebar

The clan switcher at the top of the sidebar shows the open clan and, under its
name, the games in view (**All games** or one game). It opens a search over your
clans; inside a clan with more than one game it also lets you show a single
game, which every page of that clan then follows.

The sidebar groups a clan's pages into **Workspace** (dashboard, calendar,
articles), **Operations** and, for clan admins, **Configuration** and **Bot**.
Under **Operations**, **Matches** holds matches, topic presets, tactical maps
and rosters; **Rosters** in turn holds rosters and squad presets. **Trainings**
is the other top-level operation. Under **Configuration**, **Players** contains
the player list and **Membership settings**; the latter opens the Membership
section of clan settings directly. **Tickets** likewise opens its section in
clan settings, and **Clan settings** opens the full settings hub. **Bot** links
to its Discord panels; global administrators also see **Competitions** there.

**Clan settings** shows a number when required settings are still missing
(games, the announcement channel or the clan role); it disappears once they are
set. Logi's global administrators also see **Global administration** (marked
"admins only"). The navigation scrolls independently, while the account area
stays aligned to the bottom edge of the sidebar and opens **My account**.

Pages below a section show their trail above the title, for example
**Settings › Discord › Channels and language**; each step links back. The
restart button for the guided tour, LogiComms, the wiki and Discord support
are in the footer.

## Dashboard statistics

The clan dashboard follows the game selector. With a specific game selected,
the statistic charts and recent results use only that game's recorded matches.
Recent results and win/loss totals include confirmed or corrected manually entered
scores, even without a server or statistics link. Drafts, trainings and provisional
results are excluded. Score-only results add no player statistics. The authenticated
API exposes these reviewed scores through `/api/v1/clan/result-summaries`, including
their manual origin and review status.

With **All games**, Logi keeps each supported game's statistics separate so
unrelated fields are never combined. Hell Let Loose and HLL: Vietnam show
combat effectiveness, support, K/D and points. Wardogs shows money, kills,
deaths and K/D. Games without registered statistics do not show empty or
made-up charts. All labels follow the dashboard language.

On a phone the sidebar opens from the menu button at the top left and closes
when you open a page; the avatar at the top right opens your account. Below a
section the top bar shows a back arrow to the page one level up and the page's
name instead. A page's main action, where it has one, stays in a bar at the
bottom of the screen, next to a short state such as "1 change" when the page
has one.

## When something goes wrong

A page that cannot load shows what happened, a **Try again** button and
**Details for support** (an error reference, the time and the page) that you
can copy into a support request. A save shows a short notice at the top right:
a green tick when it worked, a red outline with the reason when it did not. A
value that cannot be saved is also marked at its field, with the reason
underneath. An address that does not exist shows a page
in your language with a way back: the home page on the public site, or your
clan list inside the dashboard.

## First-time guides

The first-login tour explains clan selection, workspace modes and account settings.
If a clan you manage still needs the bot, it also explains the invitation and names
the clan on the Discord invite button.
Each clan then has a tour for your current access:

- **Full clan managers** can explore every feature area: matches, Discord sign-ups,
  rosters, attendance, results, trainings, calendars, players, performance, articles,
  presets, tactical maps, applications, tickets, teams, competitions, panels,
  seeding, game servers, recorded game history, bot commands, Wardogs League
  tracking and website integrations. If the bot is missing, a chapter
  explains the invite, role order and setup guide.
- **Competition-mode managers** see teams, players, competition registration,
  workspace settings and their account. The tour does not require a bot.
- **Members** see the pages used to follow events and published rosters; members
  in competition mode receive account and workspace guidance.

The guide opens the actual dashboard pages and spotlights the section being
explained, including its labels and related controls. Steps that ask you to click
an action highlight that specific button or link. Click the highlighted action
when prompted, or use **Next**, **Back**
and the arrow keys. **Skip tour** or Escape closes it; **Restart tour** in the
footer reopens it. Closing the guide returns you to the page where you started.

Every tour page uses a fictional NOVA clan, including calendar events, sign-ups,
rosters, results, leagues and settings. These examples also appear in an empty
clan and in competition mode. The players table includes performance trends; opening the
example player shows their recorded performance charts. Match creation uses the
same five-step form as normal creation, with a local demonstration of filling it
in. Use **Pause example** or **Replay example** to control the animation.
Reduced-motion preferences show the completed example immediately.

Examples do not create matches, change clan settings or send Discord messages.
Your real clan content returns when you leave the tour. During the guide,
other dashboard actions are blocked. Inviting the bot opens Discord for you to
complete separately.

Completion is saved to your account for each clan, role and mode. Becoming a
manager or changing from competition mode to full mode opens the corresponding
tour even if you completed a different one. Your current chapter is stored in
this browser so an unfinished tour can resume. If completion cannot be saved,
the error notice offers **Retry saving**.
