# Calendar and articles

## Calendar

The calendar is a time-based view of scheduled workspace activity. Use it when you know _when_ something happens; use the Events, Matches, or Trainings lists when you need a particular type of activity or management action.

It combines scheduled events with manual calendar items configured by managers.
Events use meeting time through game end; manual items can be all-day or timed,
and can recur weekly, monthly by date, monthly by weekday pattern, or yearly.
Recurrence keeps the original duration. It does not create separate editable
events: editing or removing the source manual item changes every future
occurrence.

The calendar only expands a finite range and caps one recurring item at 512
occurrences, so an endless recurrence is useful for normal schedules but is not
an archival record of every historical instance. Event times are displayed in
the workspace Discord timezone when it is configured. Check the meeting time
and game time separately when both are shown.

Calendar categories affect presentation (label, colour, emoji) rather than
eligibility, Discord permissions, or event status. A missing category falls
back to the normal event presentation; it does not hide the event.

## Articles

Articles are server-owned written information: announcements, guides, rules, or briefings. Open an article to read it. Managers can create articles and edit existing ones from the workspace’s Articles section.

<Steps>

### Create an article (manager)

Open **Articles** and choose the create action.

### Write for readers

Use a clear title and organise the content around what readers need to know first.

### Review the published view

Open the saved article as a reader would before sharing it.

</Steps>

### What saving an article requires

Title, description, and body are all required. Logi trims title/description,
keeps at most 20 non-empty tags, and ignores empty attachment values. Articles
are workspace-scoped and are listed newest-first by their original creation
time; updating one does not move it to the top. There is no draft or separate
public/private visibility state in this workflow: save only material that is
appropriate for everyone who can access that workspace's articles.

Deleting an article removes it rather than archiving it. Existing direct links
will no longer resolve, and Logi does not automatically remove a copied link
from Discord, another article, or an external site.

<Callout type="warning">
    Do not put Discord tokens, private channels, personal data, or other
    sensitive operational details in an article intended for broad visibility.
</Callout>
