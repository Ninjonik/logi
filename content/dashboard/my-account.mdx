# Your account

Open **My account** from the bottom of the dashboard sidebar (on a phone, from
your avatar at the top right). This area is for your own account preferences
and privacy-related actions, not for changing a server’s membership or
settings.

Every change on this page is saved as soon as you make it; there is no save
button. A short confirmation appears after each change, and a failed change is
undone on screen with a message explaining why.

The page header shows your avatar and the Discord account you signed in with.
Click the avatar (a camera appears on it) to upload a new picture. **Log out**
signs you out of this browser.

## Language and appearance

- **App language** switches the dashboard and website to that language on this
  device.
- **Theme** is light, dark or **System**, which follows your device.
- **Clan after sign-in** chooses the workspace that opens when you enter the
  dashboard. If you leave it on **Automatic**, Logi stores a default as soon as
  possible: your primary clan first, then another non-pending recruit, member,
  or reserve-member clan, then another workspace you can access. It remains
  stored so the dashboard can open it immediately; mercenary and pending
  assignments are not preferred. A stored clan you can no longer open is
  skipped, and the dashboard shows your clan list instead.

## Game accounts

**Steam** shows whether your Steam account is verified. A verified account
shows the verification date (hover it to see the Steam ID), and your
statistics are matched to it automatically; **Unlink** stops future player attribution. Without a
verified account, **Verify with Steam** signs you in on Steam to prove control
of the account.

**Epic, Xbox, PlayStation** lists the IDs you entered yourself. They are
marked as unverified because Logi cannot check them. **Add ID** (or **Edit
IDs**) opens a dialog where you can enter several IDs separated by commas. If
one of them is already linked to another player, the message appears under the
field. Platform IDs are normalised when saved; use the exact account IDs that
the game platform supplies rather than a display name.

### Linking in Discord with /link

`/link` in a clan's Discord server does the same privately, in one message that
updates as you go, in the clan's language:

1. When Logi has stored games from the clan's servers, it first asks **Have
   you played on the clan's servers?** **Yes, find me** opens **Find me in
   game**: type at least three characters of your in-game name or your exact
   ID and pick yourself from the players of those games, shown with the
   platform, when you last played (with the weekday only within the last
   week) and on which server. The clan application searches the same games. Nobody found offers **Search again** and
   **Enter ID manually**.
2. Otherwise, or with **No, I'll enter my ID**, pick the platform: Steam
   (Steam64 ID, 17 digits), Epic Games (Epic Account ID), Xbox (your gamertag
   or Xbox user ID) or PlayStation (your online ID). A short guide says what
   the ID looks like and links the platform's own instructions; the window
   asks for exactly that ID.
3. The ID is checked before it is saved. A Steam64 ID has 17 digits and starts
   with 7656119 (a `steamcommunity.com/profiles/…` link also works); anything
   else is refused with **Enter again**. An ID another player already has is
   refused too.

With accounts linked, `/link` lists them with **Add another account** and
**Unlink account**. Unlinking says what changes: statistics from that account
stop being matched, and an account your open clan application uses is marked
so you do not remove it by mistake.

An ID you link this way is your own statement, not proof that the account is
yours. To prove a Steam account, use **Verify Steam on the web**, which opens
this page.

## Bot direct messages

**Match recap** is on by default and sends you your stats from a confirmed
match; the switch saves as soon as you change it. The **Unsubscribe from
recaps** button on a recap also turns them off for your own Discord account;
its confirmation offers **Subscribe to recaps** to reverse the choice. These
responses follow your Discord language. Pending recaps are checked against your
current preference before sending; a message already being sent may still
arrive.

**Sign-up and attendance reminders** are sent by your clan according to its
own settings, so they are not switched here.

## Keep accounts connected correctly

Use the Discord account that represents you in the community. Link platform
IDs on this page or with `/link` in Discord; Logi no longer sends one-time
linking links by direct message. Do not share a sign-in link with another
person.

Steam verification must finish while the same Logi session is still valid.
Signing out of that session, signing out everywhere, or expiry stops a pending
verification from creating a verified link. Start a new verification after signing
in again. After a session-binding upgrade, older unfinished verification attempts
also need to restart; existing verified links are retained.

## Privacy and data

### Platform-ID links

Linking an ID with `/link` adds it to your account without duplicating an
existing identical ID; it may create the basic Logi user record if one does
not yet exist. Unlinking removes only that ID.

Platform IDs are a membership-application prerequisite in the Discord flow.
They are not automatically a membership approval, group assignment, Discord
role, or proof of identity. Use the membership application process when the
community requires approval.

**Download ZIP** downloads a readable report and a machine-readable copy of
your own stored data.

**Delete account** removes your profile, sign-ups and linked accounts. To
confirm, type your name into the field; the button stays disabled until the
name matches (letter case does not matter). Confirming creates a request for
the Logi team; it does not immediately delete the account in the browser
session, and the deletion cannot be undone once processed.

<Callout type="warning">
    Deleting or exporting personal data can affect what is available to you
    later. Read the confirmation carefully and contact the community or service
    operator if you are unsure.
</Callout>
