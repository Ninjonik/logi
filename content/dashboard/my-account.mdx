# Your account

Open your user settings from the account menu in the dashboard sidebar. This area is for your own account preferences and privacy-related actions, not for changing a server’s membership or settings.

You can change your avatar and platform-ID list there; **Save** in the profile
card stores your avatar, platform IDs and default workspace together. Your
Discord name and Discord ID are displayed for reference but are not editable
from Logi, and streamer status is shown for reference only. **Preferred
language** is the language picker: choosing a language switches the dashboard
and website to it on this device.
Match recap messages are enabled by default. You can turn them off globally in
your account settings and turn them back on there at any time; the switch saves
as soon as you change it. The **Unsubscribe
from recaps** button on a recap also turns them off for your own Discord account;
its confirmation offers **Subscribe to recaps** to reverse the choice. These
responses follow your Discord language. Pending recaps are checked against your
current preference before sending; a message already being sent may still arrive.
You can also choose the workspace that opens when you enter the dashboard. If
you leave it on **Automatic**, Logi stores a default as soon as possible: your
primary clan first, then another non-pending recruit, member, or reserve-member
clan, then another workspace you can access. It remains stored so the dashboard
can open it immediately; mercenary and pending assignments are not preferred.
Platform IDs are normalised when saved; use the exact account IDs that the game
platform supplies rather than a display name.

## Keep accounts connected correctly

Use the Discord account that represents you in the community. If a platform ID needs linking, Logi may provide a secure linking flow. Do not share a linking token or a sign-in link with another person.

Steam verification must finish while the same Logi session is still valid.
Signing out of that session, signing out everywhere, or expiry stops a pending
verification from creating a verified link. Start a new verification after signing
in again. After a session-binding upgrade, older unfinished verification attempts
also need to restart; existing verified links are retained.

## Privacy requests

### Platform-ID links

When the bot starts a platform-ID link, the resulting token is single-use and
expires after 15 minutes. Starting another link for the same Discord account
invalidates that account's earlier unused link tokens. Completing it adds the
trimmed platform ID to the account without duplicating an existing identical
ID; it may create the basic Logi user record if one does not yet exist.

Platform IDs are a membership-application prerequisite in the Discord flow.
They are not automatically a membership approval, group assignment, Discord
role, or proof of identity. Use the membership application process when the
community requires approval.

Logi provides routes for privacy export and privacy requests. Use your account settings or contact the service operator when you need help understanding your data options.

The export button downloads a ZIP containing a readable report and a
machine-readable copy of your own stored data. **Request account erasure** asks
you to confirm first. Confirming creates a request for the Logi team; it does
not immediately delete the account in the browser session.

<Callout type="warning">
    Deleting or exporting personal data can affect what is available to you
    later. Read the confirmation carefully and contact the community or service
    operator if you are unsure.
</Callout>
