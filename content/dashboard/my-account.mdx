# Your account

Open your user settings from the account menu in the dashboard sidebar. This area is for your own account preferences and privacy-related actions, not for changing a server’s membership or settings.

## Keep accounts connected correctly

Use the Discord account that represents you in the community. If a platform ID needs linking, Logi may provide a secure linking flow. Do not share a linking token or a sign-in link with another person.

## Privacy requests

Logi provides routes for privacy export and privacy requests. Use your account settings or contact the service operator when you need help understanding your data options.

<Callout type="warning">
    Deleting or exporting personal data can affect what is available to you
    later. Read the confirmation carefully and contact the community or service
    operator if you are unsure.
</Callout>
