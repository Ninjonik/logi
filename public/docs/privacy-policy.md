# Privacy Policy

**Last updated: 25.07.2026**

This Privacy Policy explains how Logi (the "**Service**"), developed by an informal, unregistered group of contributors (the "**Logi Team**", "**we**", "**us**"), collects, uses, stores, and shares personal data when you use the Logi web dashboard and Discord bot. It is written to comply with the EU General Data Protection Regulation ("**GDPR**").

## 1. Who Is the Data Controller

For each Discord server ("**guild**") that uses Logi, the **guild's administrators** and the **Logi Team** act as **joint controllers** of the personal data processed through that guild's use of Logi:

- The **guild administrators** decide which members are onboarded, what roles/categories are used, what ticket/application questions are asked, and how roster/attendance data is used within the community.
- The **Logi Team** determines how the Logi software itself processes and stores data (e.g. via the Convex database), and operates the Discord bot.

Contact for data protection questions: **ninjonik@igportals.eu**

## 2. What Personal Data We Collect

Based on how Logi's data model works, we process the following categories of personal data:

**a) Discord identity and profile data**
- Discord user ID, username/display name, avatar
- Guild (server) ID, guild name, guild avatar/description
- Roles and permissions within a guild (admin, member, mercenary, recruit, pending, dashboard-access flags)
- Whether a user is flagged as a "streamer" for events

**b) Membership and organizational data**
- Guild groups/subgroups a user belongs to (primary and secondary groups)
- Membership status (pending, recruit, member, mercenary) and join dates
- Assignment history (server, type, status, pause notes)

**c) Event, attendance, and roster data**
- Event sign-ups and attendance status (attending / not attending), including any group chosen at sign-up
- Roster placement (squad, role, notes, acknowledgement/confirmation flags)
- Reserve list membership and attendance acknowledgements
- Absence notices, including any reason text a member submits
- Attendance reminder logs (timestamps of reminders sent to a user)

**d) Tickets and membership applications**
- Content of support tickets and membership/mercenary applications created via Discord threads, including free-text answers to configured questions
- Who opened, closed, and (if applicable) the stated reason for closing a ticket/application
- Any transcript stored for a closed ticket/application

**e) Game/match performance statistics**
- In-game player identifiers and names (as reported by imported match data)
- Performance statistics: kills, deaths, kill/death ratio, offense/defense/support scores, weapon and cause-of-death breakdowns, time played, and related aggregate performance figures
- Scores/points derived from attendance and performance, used for internal ranking within a guild

**f) Account linking data**
- Temporary linking tokens used to connect a game-platform account to a Discord user, and their expiry/consumption status

**g) Technical data**
- Timestamps of record creation/updates
- Basic technical/log data generated by the underlying hosting and authentication infrastructure (see Section 6)

We do **not** knowingly collect special categories of data (e.g. health, religion, sexual orientation) unless a user voluntarily includes such information in free-text fields (such as an absence reason or ticket answer); we ask guild administrators not to design questions that solicit such data, and to instruct members not to volunteer it unnecessarily.

We do **not** currently process any payment information, as Logi does not offer paid features.

## 3. How We Use Personal Data

We use the data described above to:

- Authenticate users and enforce role-based access to the dashboard;
- Operate guild membership workflows (applications, onboarding, role/group assignment);
- Schedule events, manage sign-ups/attendance, and generate/publish rosters;
- Sync events, rosters, and related information with Discord (scheduled events, forum threads, announcements);
- Operate support-ticket and membership-application threads, including storing transcripts for reference;
- Import, store, and display match/game performance statistics and derive attendance/performance scores used within a guild;
- Send attendance reminders;
- Maintain and improve the security, reliability, and functionality of Logi.

## 4. Legal Bases for Processing (GDPR Art. 6)

| Processing activity | Legal basis |
|---|---|
| Discord authentication and role-based access | Performance of a contract (these Terms) / legitimate interests in operating the Service securely |
| Guild membership, groups, assignments | Legitimate interests of the guild in organizing its community; consent where the guild treats participation as opt-in |
| Event sign-ups, rosters, attendance | Legitimate interests in organizing community activities; performance of the guild's internal arrangements |
| Ticket and application content | Legitimate interests in providing support/processing applications; consent, where the user voluntarily submits the ticket/application |
| Match/game performance statistics | Legitimate interests of the guild in tracking performance for internal organization (e.g. team selection) |
| Attendance reminders | Legitimate interests in facilitating participation |
| Security/technical logs | Legitimate interests in securing the Service; legal obligation, where applicable |

Where we or a guild rely on "legitimate interests," we consider that the impact on members is limited to organizing voluntary participation in a community/gaming context, and that members can object or request deletion as described in Section 8.

## 5. Sharing of Personal Data

We share personal data with:

- **Discord**, as the platform through which Logi operates (Discord processes data under its own [Privacy Policy](https://discord.com/privacy));
- **Convex**, our database/backend hosting provider, which stores Logi's data on our behalf as a processor;
- **Members of the relevant guild**, where data is inherently visible within that guild's Discord server or dashboard (e.g. published rosters, ticket threads visible to support roles, membership status visible to admins);
- **Guild administrators**, who can access member data relevant to administering their own guild;
- Any other party, only where required by law, to protect the rights/safety of the Logi Team or others, or with your consent.

We do not sell personal data, and we do not share personal data with advertisers.

## 6. International Data Transfers

Discord and Convex may process and store data on servers located outside the EU/EEA (for example, in the United States). Where this occurs, we rely on the safeguards those providers offer for international transfers (such as Standard Contractual Clauses), as described in their respective privacy documentation:

- Discord: [https://discord.com/privacy](https://discord.com/privacy)
- Convex: refer to Convex's own privacy/security documentation

## 7. Data Retention

- **Membership, roster, and event data** is retained for as long as a guild continues to use Logi and for a reasonable period afterward to allow historical reporting, unless a guild administrator or affected user requests earlier deletion.
- **Tickets and applications** (including transcripts) are retained to preserve a record of support/application decisions, unless deletion is requested and no legitimate reason exists to retain them (e.g. dispute resolution).
- **Match/game statistics** are retained as part of a guild's historical performance record unless deletion is requested.
- **Linking tokens** are short-lived and are deleted or expire automatically after use or after their expiry time.
- If a guild removes the Logi bot or ceases using the Service, we will delete or anonymize that guild's associated data within a reasonable period upon request, subject to Section 8.

## 8. Your Rights (GDPR)

If you are located in the EU/EEA (and in many other jurisdictions with similar rights), you have the right to:

- **Access** the personal data we hold about you;
- **Rectify** inaccurate or incomplete data;
- **Erase** your data ("right to be forgotten"), subject to legitimate reasons we may have to retain certain records (e.g. resolving an open dispute);
- **Restrict** or **object** to certain processing, including processing based on legitimate interests;
- **Data portability**, where technically feasible, for data you provided to us;
- **Withdraw consent** at any time, where processing is based on consent, without affecting the lawfulness of processing before withdrawal;
- **Lodge a complaint** with your local data protection supervisory authority.

To exercise these rights, contact **ninjonik@igportals.eu** or your guild administrator (for guild-specific data such as roster/ticket content, which the guild also controls). We will respond within the timeframes required by GDPR (generally one month, extendable in complex cases).

## 9. Children's Privacy

Logi is not directed at children under 13 (or the higher minimum age required in your country or by Discord). We do not knowingly collect data from children below this age. If you believe a child has provided us with personal data, contact us so we can delete it.

## 10. Security

We take reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect personal data, including relying on Discord's authentication (OAuth) rather than storing passwords, and using access controls (roles/permissions) to limit who can view sensitive data within a guild. However, no system is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

## 11. Automated Decision-Making

Logi computes derived scores (e.g. attendance/performance scores) using configured rules, but these scores are used to inform decisions made by human guild administrators (e.g. team selection), not to make legal or similarly significant decisions about you in a fully automated way without human involvement.

## 12. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Material changes will be communicated to affected guild administrators (e.g. via the dashboard or a Discord announcement) with reasonable notice before they take effect.

## 13. Contact

For any privacy question, data request, or concern, contact: **ninjonik@igportals.eu**

You may also contact your national data protection authority. A list of EU supervisory authorities is available at: [https://edpb.europa.eu/about-edpb/about-edpb/members_en](https://edpb.europa.eu/about-edpb/about-edpb/members_en)
