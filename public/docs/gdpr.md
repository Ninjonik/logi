# GDPR and your data

Logi is designed to give players clear control over their personal data. You can request a copy of the data associated with your Logi account and request erasure from your account settings.

## Your rights

Under the GDPR, you may have rights to access, rectify, erase, restrict processing of, object to processing of, and receive a portable copy of your personal data. The exact scope of these rights can depend on the circumstances and applicable law.

## Access and export

Use the **Privacy and data** area in your dashboard settings to request a machine-readable export of the personal data associated with your signed-in account.

## Erasure requests

You can request erasure from the same area. Removing an account can also remove your active clan assignments, event sign-ups, roster placements, and other account-linked records. Some information may need to be retained where required for legal claims, security, or other lawful obligations; we will explain any limitation when handling the request.

## Contact and complaints

For questions or a request that cannot be completed in the dashboard, contact **ninjonik@igportals.eu**. You may also lodge a complaint with your competent data protection authority.

This page complements the [Privacy Policy](/en/privacy-policy), which explains the categories of data and processing in more detail.
