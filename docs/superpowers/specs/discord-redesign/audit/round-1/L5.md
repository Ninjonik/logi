The builders deliver the board:

- grey bar `#80848E`;
- "CHYBA BOTA · ZÁPAS" label;
- title, context line, retry chip, "Proč" / "Co udělat" and links;
- the full error-class table in cs, en and de;
- service status in Czech with chips and a "Změny stavu" thread that carries the outage duration;
- team-request V2 DMs in the clan colour.

The runtime wiring often does not feed them the facts they need, so many real entries reach the errors channel as "Discord akci odmítl. / Zkus to znovu…":

1. **Publication failures are wrapped and classified as "other".** This covers a match announcement, the roster, and the ticket, application and calendar panels. `publishManagedMessage` throws `PublicationNotSent{cause}` or `PublicationPermissionError`, and `discordFailureOf` (`error-reporting.ts:164-184`) reads only `error.code`/`rawError.code`, never `cause`. A missing permission is then reported as "Discord akci odmítl." with the chip "Zkusí se znovu sám", even though `PublicationPermissionError.missing` already names the permissions. The board's lead example (L5-08..10) therefore does not happen for a create or a channel pre-check.
2. **Member-role failures are reported without the role or the Discord code.** The call passes `new Error("role_unmanageable_or_deleted")` (`managed-member-roles.ts:111-117`), so it reads "other" instead of "Role @Člen je v seznamu rolí výš než role Logi…".
3. **Ticket entries miss their context.** There is no author, and intro, rename and support entries have no context line at all. Their chip says the player was told to retry, but the player in fact got a success reply.
4. **Explanation-only reports lose their text.** The panel password was hidden, and the seed control channel is public; both become "Bot nemohl dokončit akci / Discord akci odmítl." (`extra.txt`).

## Fix list (by importance)

1. **Classify wrapped publication errors** (L5-08, 09, 10, 22, 26, B02, B03). This one change fixes most of them.
   - In `discord-bot/src/error-reporting.ts:164-185` (`discordFailureOf`), unwrap `error.cause` and `error.deliveryCause`.
   - In `failureFacts` (`:264-327`), treat a `PublicationPermissionError` (`discord-bot/src/sync/publication.ts:84-93`) as `missingPermission` with `missingPermissions = error.missing` and channel `#channelName`.
   - Treat `PublicationChannelError("channel_missing")` as `unknownChannel`.
2. **Member-role failures** (L5-14, L5-21).
   - Carry the role ID and the outcome (`role_unmanageable_or_deleted` / `discord_forbidden`) from `createRoleFailureReporter` (`discord-bot/src/sync/role-failure-reports.ts`) into `reportClanDiscordError` (`sync/managed-member-roles.ts:111-117`) as `roleId` plus a Discord-like error, so that `roleAbove` or `unknownRole` is chosen.
   - Group the reports per role.
3. **Explanation-only reports** (L5-16).
   - Add sources, e.g. `panelPasswordHidden` and `seedControlPublic`, with their own title, Proč and Co udělat copy in `src/domain/discord-messages/bot-errors.ts` and `src/lib/clan-language/system.ts`.
   - Use them in `discord-bot/src/public-panels/worker.ts:314-330` and `discord-bot/src/seed/worker.ts:396-409` instead of the deprecated `action`/`scope` free text.
4. **Ticket context** (L5-12, L5-24, L5-B03).
   - In `discord-bot/src/interactions/tickets.ts`, pass `userId`, `categoryLabel` and `number` in `fail` (`:239-249`) and in the intro, rename and add-member reports (`:357-370,389-400,442-455`).
   - Give `ticketIntro`, `ticketRename` and `ticketSupport` (and the application equivalents) a retry chip and follow-up that do not claim the player was told. For example add a flow `"background"` in `BOT_ERROR_SOURCE_SPECS` (`bot-errors.ts:147-334`).
5. **Roster source** (L5-17). Report roster publish failures with `source: "roster"`, wrapping the roster `publishManagedMessage` in `discord-bot/src/sync/events.ts`, so that "Soupiska se nezveřejnila" is used.
6. **Event roles context** (L5-19). Pass `roleId` (attendee or reserve role) at `discord-bot/src/event-roles.ts:150-160`.
7. **Notice post copy** (L5-43). In `src/lib/clan-language/rosters.ts:21`, use "start {time}" in this card. Either use a separate key for `attendanceNoticeView`, because "start ve" is right for other cards, or change the shared key if every board shows "start 20:00". Consider dropping the divider before "Docházka na webu".
8. **Team link** (L5-39, L5-40). Deep-link to the team, or record the accepted list target (`team-request-notifications.ts:44-61`).
9. **Cosmetic** (L5-08). Render the error card's context line muted (`-#`, or a meta block) rather than as a header subtitle (`bot-errors.ts:828`).
