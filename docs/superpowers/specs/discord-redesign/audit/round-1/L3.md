Every L3 panel exists in Components V2 through the kit, edits itself in place and is reachable at runtime:
- calendar;
- competition table (one message per division);
- results, with the last-5 backfill;
- live score and server status;
- WD League, through the P6 panels;
- "Nahlásit hráče".

The interactions are registered in `discord-bot/src/interactions/features.ts` (`panelInteractions`). The worker is started in `discord-bot/src/index.ts`.

The remaining gaps:
- In the default style A, the generated image ignores the "Skóre" (server status) and "Nejlepší hráči" switches.
- The results panel's compact look cannot be chosen in the dashboard editor.
- Private replies ("Zobrazit hráče", "Nahlásit hráče") ignore a custom clan colour.
- Some visual simplifications forced by Discord: no team logos in text, and `<t:f>` timestamps.

## Fix list (by importance)

1. **Score image ignores the "Skóre" and "Nejlepší hráči" switches (L3-33, L3-36, L3-44).** `discord-bot/src/public-panels/panel-runner.ts:517-536` builds `liveScoreImageModel` without the layout or leader switches. `src/domain/discord-publications/live-panel-image.ts:72-120` always fills `leaders` and the scores. As a result, a "Stav serveru" panel (score off) or a panel with "Nejlepší hráči" off still shows the score and top 3 in the default style A. Add the switches to the model (`panel-image-model.ts`) and the layout (`src/lib/panel-image/layout.tsx`).
2. **Compact results look not selectable (L3-31, L3-B05).** Add the switch to the results panel in `src/components/app/discord-panels/editor-steps.tsx` and `panel-editor.tsx`, with i18n in cs, en and de.
3. **Private replies ignore a custom clan colour (L3-39, L3-58).** In `discord-bot/src/public-panels/interactions.ts:136,139` and `discord-bot/src/player-reports.ts:203,528,530,675,680`, pass `style` (the clan `messageStyle`), as `discord-bot/src/seed/interactions.ts:118` does.
4. **Low priority:**
   - The admin-paused chip is amber in the League panel (L3-54; kit `pausedState` tone `warning` in `src/domain/discord-messages/message-layout.ts:105-118`).
   - The calendar match type has no round (L3-14).
   - Status mode shows the time left (L3-43).
