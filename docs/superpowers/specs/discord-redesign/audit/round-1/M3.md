## Reported deviation checked: "Online · zastaralé" unreachable

Verified, and **not** caused by a Discord limit. The view supports the chip: `src/domain/discord-commands/server-status-view.ts:83-101` gives "Online · zastaralé" for `state: "online"` + `freshness: "stale"`, rendered in `render-status.png`, right card. The rows come from `gameData:listConnections`, which passes every connection through `projectSnapshot` (`src/domain/game-data/policy.ts:27-60`):

- `state` is forced to `"unknown"` whenever `freshness !== "fresh"` (lines 47-50). A stale row therefore always reads "Zastaralé" (left card, row 2).
- Data at least 15 minutes old is `freshness: "unavailable"` (lines 33-35). The board's own example, "před 25 min · RCON", would show **"Bez dat"**, not "Online · zastaralé".

This is a Logi data-projection choice. Fix: expose the last observed state for stored-status readers, for example `lastState: observation?.state ?? null` on the snapshot or in `listConnections` (`convex/gameData.ts:154-185`). Then use it in `serverStatusRows` (`discord-bot/src/interactions/server-status.ts:33-54`) for stale rows, and decide with the owner whether data older than 15 minutes should keep its last state plus "zastaralé" or show "Bez dat".

Map it in `serverStatusRows` (`discord-bot/src/interactions/server-status.ts:33-54`) so stale rows read "Online · zastaralé" / "Offline · zastaralé". Decide with the owner whether data older than 15 minutes shows the last state plus "zastaralé" (board) or "Bez dat" (today).
2. **`/close_ticket` admin-fixable failures (M3-07, M3-B02).** In `discord-bot/src/interactions/close-ticket.ts:190-224`, detect failed rename, lock, archive and card post.
