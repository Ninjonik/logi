- P6-B05 · Message length ≤4000 characters; overflow shows fewer fixtures plus "how many more".
- P6-B06 · Refresh: fixtures every 60 s; table and results on each new league result.
- P6-B07 · Messages posted once in a fixed order, then edited; channel reserved for the bot.
- P6-B08 · Recent results: placements 1–3 within the last 7 days, unverified by Logi.
- P6-B09 · Results panels: one per game (second one blocked in the editor); backfill the last 5 confirmed results oldest-first on creation; then one card per confirmed result; corrections edit.
- P6-B10 · Results panel must not require a game-server connection (keyed by game).
- P6-B11 · The clan's team is marked subtly (bold, white mark, "›" in the table).
