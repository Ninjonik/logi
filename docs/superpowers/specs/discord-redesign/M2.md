## 2. Behaviour and data

- M2-B01 · `/help`: ephemeral, no options, filters commands by the caller's permission groups (member, category support, Logi admin), links to the wiki commands page; shows a setup notice when the guild has no Logi workspace.
- M2-B02 · `/stats`: ephemeral; replies and shared card in the clan language; three inline tiles; selected view primary; each view re-reads fresh data; no "Obnovit".
- M2-B03 · Share: with a default share channel (or `kanál` option) posts directly; otherwise asks with a channel select; requires the requester and the bot to send messages and embed links; shared card names the sharer and the data time.
- M2-B04 · Coverage sentence when some games lack a metric ("Zabití a K/D známe z 21 z 23 her.").
- M2-B05 · Numbers formatted in the clan locale (e.g. "1 284", "1,37").
- M2-B06 · `/player`: ephemeral; "Sdílet" posts publicly in the command's channel; profile names game and source; autocomplete shows name and clan status in Czech.
- M2-B07 · Empty and disabled states give the next step (longer period, who can enable it).
